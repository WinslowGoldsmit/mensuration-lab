Unwrapping a Cylinder

Live GeoGebra model — drag r and h, then hide the top/bottom circles to isolate the curved surface as it unrolls.

Interactive model

To hide the top/bottom circles: use the Algebra panel on the left of the applet — click the small circle (⚪) icon next to each cap object to toggle its visibility off, so only the curved lateral surface remains as it unrolls. You can also right-click an object directly on the 3D view and choose "Show Object."

Question

With the caps hidden, set radius = 3 cm and height = 7 cm, unroll the curved surface, and read off the resulting rectangle's dimensions. What is the curved surface area?

Hint: width = 2πr, height = h → CSA = 2πrh
